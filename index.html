    Model rules and limits

    Plan JSON has title and tasks. Each task has exactly id, label, minutes and needs. IDs begin with a letter and use letters, digits, underscores or hyphens, up to 20 characters. Title/labels use 1–80 printable UTF-16 units without edge whitespace. Up to eight unique predecessors per task; missing IDs, duplicate IDs and unknown fields are rejected. Source limit 30,000 text units.

    Only finish-to-start edges with zero lag are supported. Cycles, including self-cycles, block schedules and exports and show one closed witness. All zero-slack tasks are marked critical; ties can produce multiple critical paths. One deterministic chain is shown as an example, not a list of every path. Latest timings are relative to the computed common finish.

    Scenario adds 0–1,440 minutes to one original duration, without mutating the baseline. A derived duration may therefore reach 2,880 minutes. No calendar dates, resource leveling, task execution, shared-worker constraints, progress tracking or schedule guarantees. No application storage or analytics; browser session restoration is outside app control.
